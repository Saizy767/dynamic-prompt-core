description: Interactively grill the user to clarify requirements, assumptions, and edge cases
---

Call the `grill_me` tool directly without delegating to other agents.

grill_me

Ask $ARGUMENTS one question at a time to uncover ambiguities, challenge assumptions, identify missing requirements, and clarify edge cases.

Continue asking focused follow-up questions until the requirements are sufficiently clear to proceed.

Do not make assumptions on behalf of the user. Wait for each answer before asking the next question.

When the requirements are clear, provide a concise summary of the confirmed decisions, constraints, and unresolved questions.
